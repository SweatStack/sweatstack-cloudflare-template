# Privacy policy

_Last updated: [date]_

{{APP_NAME}} is run by **[your name or company]**. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.
Questions about your data: **[your email address]**.

## What we receive from SweatStack

When you sign in with SweatStack, you choose to share data with this app. We receive your name and your
activities: sport, time, duration, distance, heart rate, power and speed, and the data recorded during them.
We use this data only to show your training back to you.

## What we store

- Your SweatStack user id and name.
- Encrypted access tokens that let the app read your data from SweatStack.
- If you turn on notifications: your browser's push subscription, so we can tell you about new activities.
- A log of recent notification events (activity ids only), kept for 14 days for troubleshooting.

We don't store copies of your activities: they are read from SweatStack when you open a page.
We don't use analytics or advertising, and we don't sell or share your data.

## Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. Another is used for a few minutes while you sign in. There are no tracking cookies.

## Who processes your data

- **Cloudflare** runs this app and its database.
- **SweatStack** holds your training data. See the [SweatStack privacy policy](https://app.sweatstack.no/legal/privacy).
- Your browser's **push service** (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) delivers notifications.

## Your choices

- **Disconnect** on the Settings page deletes everything this app stores about you.
- You can revoke this app's access in your SweatStack account at any time.
- For any other request about your data, contact **[your email address]**.
